# Product (authenticated app) UX

- The `product-screen` skill is MANDATORY for any create/edit here — a `PreToolUse` hook (`.claude/hooks/product-screen-guard.mjs`) loads it automatically; you never wait to be asked.
- **Goal, not table**: design from the user's job and what success looks like. Never default to a CRUD table of rows — that shape is allowed only when the job really is scan/compare/manage many rows.
- **Never a blank screen**: every zero-data view uses `EmptyState` (icon + why + the ONE next action). A brand-new user must find a path to value (the aha moment).
- **Less text on the screens, and no empty space** (the owner's rule of 2026-11-09, brought from ONE): a page, a card or a section opens on its title, and what it is for sits behind the "i" beside it (`description` of `PageHeader` and `SectionTitle`, or an `InfoHint` beside a card's own title until `CardTitle` is packaged; 200 characters at most in both languages, kept by `lib/i18n/hint-budget.test.ts`). In sight stays only state or data (`detail`: what an app or a recipe does, a meeting's text in its console, when the metrics were read) and what must be read before acting (what the AI asks before it installs, what the billing data is used for, values the site shows to anybody). A definition that repeats its title goes (the e-mails card, the caption over the access's list); a row that opens a record shows it by its pointer and hover, never by a sentence; a tile is its icon and its name. A section or list with nothing in it takes a drawing of `@/icons/illustrations` (`EmptyState` `illustration`): the catalog and its filters, the recipes, the news, the meetings and the next ones, the roadmap (one band when every column is empty), the machines, a guide still to come, and the consoles' lists; a state of permission (a guide closed to the plan), of a record that does not exist or of a read that failed keeps its icon.
- **Onboarding never diverts attention**: `/onboarding` and any `SetupWizard` carry nothing that leads away from finishing them (no link to another screen, no "see more", no list of things to explore); each step does its work in place, and what is information for later lives on `/inicio` or its own page. **Prerequisites come first**: whatever a later step cannot be done without (the second factor, a connection, a fact the database checks) is its own step before the first step that needs it.
- **Who does the work, here**: the member's AI runs every flyee command, following `/instalar.md` (the member never uses the terminal), and the platform never runs AI on a member's behalf (the club never spends AI credit for them). So what the product "does alone" in this member area is a rule, a data read or the platform's own job (rotating the token, publishing a version, ringing the bell), said as the platform's or the AI's work, never as a step the member owes; what the member does is confirm in the conversation, approve in the browser, choose and pay.
- **A card asks only for what is still owed**: a request already fulfilled leaves the card's main area, said as done, with doing it again one click away (`QuickActions`, under `template` in `components/product/catalog.json`); what is still owed is one line in the attention colour with its one button. Never hide a right the member must find in plain sight (deleting their data, revoking a machine) in that menu.
- **State in icon, colour and words**: done `success` with a check, under way `warning` with a clock (never the primary yellow, a fill), to look at or act on `accent-3` with an exclamation, failure `error` (kept for failures), information grey; the words say the state too (`StatusChip`; `CardTitle` and `StateLine` under `template`). An act the member must understand is confirmed in an `OutcomeDialog` (template), never in an `Alert` at the top of the page.
- **A Pro feature is never shown disabled**, whatever the playbook says of a module the account does not have: it stays enabled and opens the upgrade prompt on the click (`apps/web/CLAUDE.md`, "FlyeeClub Pro on the click"); a Pro area with nothing published says "Em breve".
- Long setups use `SetupWizard` (progressive disclosure); steps to first result use `OnboardingChecklist` + `@flyee/onboarding` (state only; step definitions live in the project's code).
- **Engagement**: completion drive (`ActivationProgress`) and competence feedback only. Never ship points, badges, leaderboards or streaks — documented failures, and streaks carry regulatory risk.
- MUI v9 + theme tokens only, next-intl for every string, inline form errors near the field, skeletons that reserve space.
- **Semantic fields**: phone, CPF/CNPJ, CEP and money never go in a raw `TextField` — use `components/product/fields/*` (mask + check-digit validation + CEP auto-fill via `@flyee/fields`); persist `onlyDigits()`, never the masked string.
